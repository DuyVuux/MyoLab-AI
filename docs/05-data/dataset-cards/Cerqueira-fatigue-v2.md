## Observed facts
The official record describes raw sEMG from 13 healthy adults, eight muscles, twelve
upper-body dynamic movements and self-perceived fatigue ratings. Raw sEMG is recorded at
1259 Hz. The fatigue rating is external self-report evidence; it is not a clinical fatigue
diagnosis and does not validate MotionLab fatigue behavior.

## Role
- non-stationarity/fatigue-context research;
- later metric sensitivity exploration;
- external domain supportability tests.
