# DAY33 Acceptance Criteria — Research Benchmark Corpus v1

DAY33 passes engineering validation only when all conditions below are true.

1. At least one public source has verified primary-source license/provenance.
2. Public raw payload is not committed to the repository/handoff ZIP.
3. A reproducible synthetic known-truth corpus exists and uses DAY22 WindowIdentity.
4. Every payload item uses a DAY32 evidence tier without automatic promotion.
5. Synthetic low-amplitude stress is not labeled as stroke/paresis/atrophy.
6. Development and locked partitions have disjoint seeds.
7. Locked truth is not present in the DAY33 public manifest.
8. Every signal artifact is content-hashed and synthetic/non-patient.
9. Dataset cards state source, DOI/license, metadata, role and limitations.
10. No training/model fitting is authorized or executed by DAY33.
11. Clinical validation remains `NOT_PERFORMED`.
12. Claim scope remains `RESEARCH_ONLY`.
